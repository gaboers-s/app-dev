# I Was Caught Up in a Hero Summoning, but That World Is at Peace

## About the Novel

**I Was Caught Up in a Hero Summoning, but That World Is at Peace** (*Yuusha Shoukan ni Makikomareta kedo, Isekai wa Heiwa deshita*) is a Japanese light novel written by **Toudai**.

The story follows **Miyama Kaito**, a young man who accidentally gets summoned into another world alongside other people. However, unlike typical isekai stories, the world is already peaceful, and there is no Demon Lord that needs to be defeated.

## Story Summary

The story begins when **Miyama Kaito** gets caught up in a hero summoning. Instead of fighting monsters or saving the world, he discovers that the world is already at peace.

Throughout his journey, Kaito meets different characters, including powerful beings, gods, and demons. He slowly builds relationships with them while discovering more about the world.

## Main Characters

Some of the important characters include:

1. **Miyama Kaito** - The main protagonist who was accidentally summoned.
2. **Kuro (Kuromueina)** - A kind and powerful demon who becomes close to Kaito.
3. **Isis Remnant** - One of the Six Kings, known as the Death King.
4. **Alice** - A mysterious and playful character.
5. **Shallow Vernal** - A powerful goddess who plays an important role in the story.

## Why I Like This Novel

I like this novel because it is different from the usual isekai stories. Instead of focusing mainly on battles and defeating enemies, it focuses more on **relationships, character development, romance, and peaceful adventures**.


## My Personal Rating

**Rating: 10/10**

*I recommend this novel to readers who enjoy fantasy, romance, and relaxing stories with interesting characters.*

> Not every hero summoning needs a hero to save the world. Sometimes, the adventure is about the people you meet along the way.
